🪂 Parachute

Analog career exercises, rebuilt for the browser.

Interactive remakes of the pen-and-paper self-assessment worksheets popularized by What Color Is Your Parachute? — faster to work through, with the tallying and ranking done for you. No sign-up, works offline, saved in your browser.
